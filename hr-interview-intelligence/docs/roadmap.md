# Roadmap

## Stage 1: MVP

Duration target: 4 to 8 weeks.

Deliverables:

- Resume upload and parsing.
- Interview audio upload.
- Whisper-compatible transcript generation.
- JD matching.
- AI scoring.
- Basic HR dashboard.

## Stage 2: Smart AI Enhancement

Duration target: 4 to 6 weeks.

Deliverables:

- Behavioral interview analysis.
- Confidence and communication analytics.
- Personality detection as optional onboarding signal.
- Mentor recommendation.
- Score explanations and evidence review.

## Stage 3: Enterprise

Deliverables:

- Multi-company support.
- Role permissions.
- Audit logs.
- Bias detection and model monitoring.
- Approval workflow.
- ATS integration.
- Compliance export reports.
